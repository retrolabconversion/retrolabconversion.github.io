GOOD TO KNOW

Privacy & downloads

This site adds no advertising trackers, analytics or embedded video players. YouTube links open the channel only when you choose to visit.

Once connected, the catalogue reads public release information from GitHub. Your browser then contacts GitHub, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Release information may be cached in this browser tab for five minutes.

Download counts come from GitHub for each published file. They are not counts of unique people and do not guarantee completed transfers. If a file is replaced, its counter may restart.

The creator workspace can save an optional local draft in your browser. It never uploads files or stores account credentials.
